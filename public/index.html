Project description

ACCESS4ALL is a research project led by Dr. Iskander De Bruycker and funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO) and the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences at Maastricht University. Our team includes Sandra Martinez Böhme and Susanna Eiselt (PhD Researchers), and Britt Vande Walle (Assistant Professor).

 

ACCESS4ALL investigates the issue, delving into the question:

“How do lobbyists’ socio-demographic backgrounds impact their access to policymakers, and why?”
