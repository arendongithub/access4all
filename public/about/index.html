Project

In a context of heightened geopolitical pressures and heightened competition for technological dominance, the EU has adopted a series of instruments to protect itself from economic coercion. Yet, internal divisions are becoming acute when implementing these new measures. So, what drives support or opposition to geoeconomic policies in the EU?

 

Geoeconomic FaultLines examines how the geoeconomic turn is transforming the coalitional politics within the EU. Our research focuses on mapping and explaining the political cleavages activated by these new instruments. By understanding these patterns of consensus and contestation, we aim to identify the political barriers for the EU to develop strategic autonomy in a world of mounting competition..

Team

Iskander De Bruycker

Associate Professor of EU Politics and Policy
Maastricht University View profile
Iskander De Bruycker serves as Program Director of the MA European Public Affairs programme. He is also the Principal Investigator of the NWO-funded VIDI project ACCESS4ALL, which examines inequalities in access to policymakers. His research focuses on European public affairs, EU lobbying, political communication, and interest group politics. His work has been published in leading journals, including the European Journal of Political Research, Journal of Common Market Studies, West European Politics, and Political Communication.

Britt Vande Walle

Assistant Professor in European Public Affairs
Maastricht University View profile
After completing her PhD at KU Leuven as a Doctoral Fellow of the Research Foundation Flanders (FWO), she started working on the ACCESS4ALL project. Her main research interests include comparative politics and political organisations (political parties, think tanks, interest groups). She is particularly interested in the far right and how political organisations perceive them. Her work has been published in political science journals including Political Studies, Government & Opposition, and Acta Politica.

Susanna Eiselt

PhD Candidate in Political Science
Maastricht University View profile
Susanna is part of the ACCESS4ALL project. Her dissertation focuses on the knowledge, skills, and experience of EU public affairs professionals and how this informs their access to policymakers. She holds a Master’s degree in Socio-Ecological Economics and Policy from the Vienna University of Economics and Business.

Sandra Martinez Böhme

PhD Candidate in Political Science
Maastricht University View profile
Sandra is part of the ACCESS4ALL project. Her dissertation focuses on the political ideology of EU public affairs professionals and how this informs their representative role and access to policymakers. She holds a Research Master degree in European Studies from Maastricht University where she specialised in democracy and representation as well as quantitative methods.
