In the eyes of the public, lobbyists are usually shadowy figures who influence politics from behind the scenes. Little is known about them as individuals. Who are the lobbyists who seek to influence EU politics? Speakers are: Iskander de Bruycker (PhD, Associate Professor EU Policy), Britt Vande Walle (PhD, Assistant Professor of EU Policy), Susanna Eiselt (PhD Researcher in political science) and Sandra Martinez-Böhme (PhD Researcher in political science).
